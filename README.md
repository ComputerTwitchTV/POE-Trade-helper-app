# PoE Trade Helper

Turn a Path of Exile build guide into a shopping list. Paste a build, click a
piece of gear, and the trade site opens with that item's stats already filled in.

## Download

**[Download PoE-Trade-Helper.exe](https://github.com/ComputerTwitchTV/POE-Trade-helper-app/releases/latest/download/PoE-Trade-Helper.exe)** (Windows 10 or 11)

Put it anywhere you like, such as your Desktop, and double-click it. It opens
in your web browser. Close that browser tab to close the app.

### "Windows protected your PC"

The first time, Windows may show this because the app is new. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Using it

1. Paste a build link and press Enter. Links from maxroll.gg, mobalytics.gg,
   pobb.in and pastebin.com work, or paste a Path of Building code.
2. Pick the stage you're at (Early, Mid, Late...).
3. Click a piece of gear, then **Search the shop**.
4. **Price everything** prices the whole set at once.

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is out, it downloads it in the
background and uses it the next time you open the app. Nothing to do.

## Your data

Your settings and saved builds stay on your PC, in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\PoE Trade Helper`. The app only talks to pathofexile.com,
the build sites you paste, and this page to check for updates.
